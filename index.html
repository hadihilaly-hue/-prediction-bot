Is it actually working?

Profit alone can be luck. These checks ask whether the bot is better than Kalshi's own prices.

How much evidence is there?

Closing-price gain (CLV) is the main evidence: it shows up long before profit stops being luck. A result counts once 50 bets have it and the whole 95% range is above zero.

Injuries during the hold

Did injury news between entry and kickoff cost us? A bet is flagged when a key-position player went Out or Doubtful in that window. “Unknown” means we had no injury coverage for that game yet — ESPN only shows the current list, so the backfill can only tag open positions and historical bets stay unknown.

College football has no league-wide injury report, so we use a proxy: the book consensus moving 4¢+ against our side before kickoff. It is not real injury data and is never merged with the NFL rows above.

Pre-entry gate check: the gate runs in log mode, so bets it flagged still went through. If the flagged group shows worse CLV with 20+ bets, we flip to block.

Credit cadence

Are we spending the month's ~500 odds credits on the right days? Weight is the day's share of remaining budget: the calendar base times any cluster bump or dead-zone cut, blended up to ±50% with how many mapped games sit in the pull window (dyn). Pulled is what the slots actually spent. On/after the 20th, an under-spend month lets slots pull extra feeds.
